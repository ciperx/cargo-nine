# Audio credits

Every clip below ships inside `game/audio/` and is used under the licence stated on its source
page. Nothing here is a rip from a commercial game.

The four gunshot clips are the only non-CC0 assets. CC-BY 3.0 permits redistribution and reuse in a
game as long as the author is credited, which this file does; the credit line is repeated in the
kit's `CREDITS.md` next to the source tree so a reader of the entry sees it too. The originals were
re-encoded to mono 24 kHz Vorbis (ffmpeg) to keep the entry small; no other edits were made.
